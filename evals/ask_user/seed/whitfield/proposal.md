Prepared for Whitfield Housing, 3 November 2026.

## Background

Whitfield's repairs data lives in three systems that don't agree with each other. Before
anything is built, they want to know which questions the data can answer today, and what it
would take to answer the rest.

Four weeks. Interviews with the repairs and finance teams, a data audit of the three systems,
and a written report with a recommended roadmap.

Price: £8,500

### Option B: Discovery and prototype

Everything in Option A, plus a working dashboard prototype on two of the three systems, built
with the repairs team over a further three weeks.

Price: £14,200
